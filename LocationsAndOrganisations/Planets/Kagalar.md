# Kagalar
- **Type:** Gaia World
- **Diameter:** 13 km
- **Atmosphere:** Breathable
- **Average Temperature:** 10°C
- **Day Length:** 24 hours
- **Gaia Metrics:**
  - **Forest area:** 40%
  - **Ocean coverage:** 70%
  - **Continents:** Arokor, Kregsorin, Febelen

Kagalar is the capital of the Kagalarian Empire. It is the most populated planet in the Kagalarian Empire. Many corporations and businesses thrive here. The planet has several spacecraft construction sites in orbit.

## Jovikal
Jovikal is the capital city of the Kagalarian Empire. It is a large commercial, militaristic, and political city, with an area of 80,000 hectares. Located near the center of the continent of Kregsorin, the city sees snow often.

Given its militaristic and political traits, it is very common to see patrol warships looming overhead. When night falls, they leave their silhouette and navigation lights as the only indication that they're there. Other than patrollers, commercial spacecraft fly above land traffic playing pre-recorded advertising voicelines, some even with a large television mounted on their sides.

During the winter, it can get as cold as -30 degrees Celsius. It gets so cold that snow falls onto the ground, making the city seem ethereal and unlike others. However, because of the heat generated from plasma-based machinery, the snow doesn't gather on the streets and railroads.

### Main military base
The main operational base of the Empire is built inside a tall, rocky hill 20 kilometres to the north. It reaches 3 floors deep from the surface, each floor being 400 meters in width and length.

To protect the base from bombardment, the entrance is camouflaged by several strategically placed trees and vines, and the first underground level is 10 meters below a reinforced heat-resistant concrete mixture.

Surrounding the hill, there are 8 APML systems and 8 anti-spacefighter repeater stations, each station manned by four soldiers.

At ground level, there is a lobby with several elevators serving every floor of the base. One level underground, there are the office workers. Two levels underground, there is the hospital and the medical logistics center. Three levels underground, there is the political office and the intelligence center. Each underground level has an escape route for emergency purposes.

### Horinsoler
Horinsoler is a large commercial and military city located in southern Kregsorin, about 50,000 hectares in size. Tall buildings and skyscrapers dominate the city.

Military warships can be seen flying overhead patrolling the city, while some commercial spacecraft can be seen flying overhead while they play pre-recorded advertising voicelines.
