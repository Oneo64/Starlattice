# Timeline
## 853
The Seenian Empire was created on the planet of Ramalosedot.

## 858
The Ramalosedotian Peace Group was created.

## 1246
Eventually, as the Ramalosedotian Peace Group rose to power, they transitioned into the Kagalarian Empire and began to shift its main focus from protecting the planet to expanding its reign.

## 1400s
Mrokek and mroken were discovered by deep-earth miners.

## 1500s
After discovering the first habitable planet outside of Ramalosedot's solar system, the Kagalarians were the first to take the riskiest move by asking one of the most powerful sorcerers to create a portal to that planet. When they arrived, they found the climate to be colder than Ramalosedot's climate. Nevertheless, they named the planet (Kagalar) after themselves.

## 1913
Plasmatic weapons were invented.

## 1954
Spacecraft was invented.

## 1960
The first battleship of the Kagalarian Empire began its construction.

## 1970
Ten years later, the Kagalarians have finished their battleship.

## 1993
The first space battle happens.

## 2000
The first power armour was invented.

## 2009
Power armour was first used in battle.

## 2010
The Kingdom of Ramalosedot was conquered by the Seenian Empire.

## 2012
The Battle of Samandol was fought on the planet Ramalosedot, an attempt by Kagalar and Escor to regain control of the planet. The invasion failed.

## 2034
Since Ramalosedot was like a national treasure to the Kagalarian Empire, they began another invasion of the planet. The Battle of Mevollorvet lasted several weeks and resulted in Kagalarian victory.

## 2040
Present date.
